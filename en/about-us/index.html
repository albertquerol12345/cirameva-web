About us

CIRAMEVA is a robotics and automation company built on artificial intelligence.

  1. We turn artificial intelligence into finished work: any task a person does today in front of a computer, we do with AI agents, faster and cheaper, and we stand behind the result.
  2. We don't sell software or hours: we sell delivered results —a bid ready to submit, a report, a website, a process that runs— at a fixed price.
  3. We work for anyone who pays for office work today: public bodies, companies and individuals, in any sector, in any language, across Europe.
  4. Every job we deliver leaves behind a recipe and an automatic checker, so the next one comes out faster, cheaper and better.
  5. People decide, sign and answer for it; agents execute; human review is our quality seal.

What we work with

We use AI models from European and US providers on professional plans that do not train on your data (Anthropic, OpenAI and Google; ElevenLabs for audio transcription). Every delivery goes through an automatic checker and a person's review.

What we don't do

  • Sign as lawyers, tax advisers, auditors, engineers, doctors or sworn translators.
  • Make decisions about people: hiring, dismissing or appraising.
  • Send unsolicited sales emails or make cold calls on your behalf.
  • Invent data, sources, reviews or testimonials.
