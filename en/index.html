Frequently asked questions

What kind of work do you do?

Any office or digital work that a person does today in front of a computer: documents, data, reports, translations, websites, customer service, admin processes, bids and automation projects. When a job needs the signature of a licensed professional, we prepare the documents and that professional signs.

How can you price a job without knowing us?

From what you tell us in the form: what needs doing, in what format and by when. If we need more to fix the price, we ask in our reply. The price is fixed: it doesn't change if we take longer.

Who reviews what the agents do?

A person reviews every delivery before it is sent, helped by an automatic checker that verifies data, calculations and sources.

What don't you do?

We don't sign as lawyers, tax advisers, auditors, engineers, doctors or sworn translators, and we don't make decisions about people. We prepare the work so that the right person decides and signs.

Where do you work, and in which languages?

Remotely, for clients across Spain and the rest of Europe, in English, Spanish, Catalan, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and other European languages.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer: 50% when you accept the quote and 50% on delivery; jobs under €100 are paid on acceptance. Invoices are issued to your company or to you.
