IT, software and integrations

Many processes fail at the seams between programs: someone copies data from one to another. We connect them with integrations, software robots and custom AI agents, with the code and documentation in your name.

What we do

  • Integration between your programs via API: email, CRM, invoicing, online shop.
  • Custom AI agents that read documents, fill in systems and alert a person when something does not add up.
  • Robotic process automation (RPA) for programs without an API.
  • Small internal applications and forms.
  • Data migration between programs with validation.
  • Technical documentation of systems and code.
  • Automated testing and code review.
  • Inventory of software, licences and access rights.

What we deliver

  • Integration or automation working in your environment
  • Source code and documentation in your name
  • User and maintenance manual
  • Test report
  • Systems inventory
What we don't do. We do not keep your credentials beyond the project and all the code stays in your name. For critical systems, we work first in a test environment.
What happens if the automation fails?

Every automation keeps a log and alerts a person when something does not add up. In the quote we set the adjustment period included after delivery.

Who owns the code?

You do: we hand it over with its documentation and with no dependency on us.

Data, reports and dashboards

Data is often stuck in PDFs, in emails or scattered across several spreadsheets. We turn it into clean sheets, automated reports and dashboards, using AI agents and a person who checks the results.

What we do

  • Converting PDFs, scans and photos into clean spreadsheets.
  • Cleaning, unifying and deduplicating databases.
  • Automatic periodic reports sent by email.
  • Dashboards of indicators connected to your data.
  • Market studies and sector reports with public sources cited.
  • Surveys: design, tabulation and analysis.
  • Statistical analysis explained in plain language.
  • Combining data from several programs into a single view.

What we deliver

  • Clean spreadsheet with a change log
  • Indicator dashboard
  • Automated periodic report
  • Market study with sources
  • Survey analysis
What we don't do. Every figure in a report comes with its source. If a data point does not exist or a source is missing, we say so rather than filling the gap.
What tools do you use?

Excel, Google Sheets, Python and the usual dashboard tools. We deliver in the one you already use.

Does my data leave the EU?

We use AI providers on a professional tier; transfers and their safeguards are set out in the privacy policy and in the data processing agreement.

Web, design and accessibility

A website has to explain clearly what you do, load fast, show up in search engines and be usable by everyone. We build it with AI agents and human review, with the domain and accounts in your name.

What we do

  • Brochure website ready in 72 hours.
  • Website copy and product pages written with search engines in mind.
  • Accessibility audit against the European standard and correction of the failures.
  • Accessibility statement.
  • Maintenance and content updates.
  • Online shop: catalogue, product pages and setup.
  • Speed, technical SEO and structured data.
  • Website forms and automations.

What we deliver

  • Website published on your domain
  • Accessibility report with each failure and its fix
  • Accessibility statement
  • Copy and product pages
  • Technical SEO report
What we don't do. The domain and accounts stay in your name. No third-party cookies unless you ask for them, and in that case with the proper notice.
Is my company required to have an accessible website?

Since June 2025, the European Accessibility Act requires many websites and online shops that sell to consumers to be accessible; microenterprises providing services are exempt. In the free review we tell you whether it affects you.

Can I edit the website myself afterwards?

Yes: we show you how and, if you prefer, we maintain it for you.
