Capabilities

Robotics and industrial automation

Software robots (RPA) in your programs, and collaborative robotics and machine vision projects with integrators: we do the office side and choose with you who installs.

Software robots and AI agents

For processes that live in your programs: a software robot works the way a person would, without getting tired and keeping a log.

  • Software robots (RPA) that use your programs the way a person would: copying data, downloading documents, filling in forms.
  • AI agents that read emails, PDFs and forms, decide with your rules and alert a person when something doesn't add up.
  • API integrations between your ERP, CRM, invoicing and online shop.
  • Documentation, a log of every run and a manual so your team can maintain it.

Collaborative robotics and machine vision

For the physical processes of your plant or laboratory we do the office side of the project, end to end:

  • Analysis of the workstation and the task you want to automate, using your production data.
  • Feasibility and payback study with your costs, flagged as an assumption wherever data is missing.
  • Technical specification and requests for quotation to robotics integrators.
  • Comparison of offers: scope, safety, lead times, maintenance and terms.
  • Applications for digitalisation and industrial automation grants.
  • Operating manuals, procedures and training for the people who will work with the robot.
