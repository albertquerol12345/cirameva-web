Administration and operations

Day-to-day administrative work swallows your diary: emails, minutes, files, paperwork, deadlines. We organise and automate it with AI agents, and a person reviews what comes out.

What we do

  • Sorting and draft replies for the administration mailbox.
  • Meeting minutes and follow-up of agreed actions from recordings or notes.
  • Process map and step-by-step written procedures.
  • Document management: sorting, renaming and filing with clear criteria.
  • Online forms and formalities prepared with the information you give us.
  • Automation of repeated tasks between email, spreadsheets and your software.
  • Tracking of contracts, expiry dates and renewals.
  • Activity indicators and their regular report.

What we deliver

  • Process map and procedures
  • Minutes with a list of actions and owners
  • Organised document archive with its index
  • Documented automations
  • Calendar of deadlines
What we don't do. Formalities that require your electronic signature are signed by you: we leave them prepared for you to review and sign.
Where do we start?

With the process that takes up the most hours. You tell us about it, we map it out and give you a fixed price to automate it or do it for you.

Do I have to give you access to my systems?

Only to what is essential and with limited permissions. If you prefer, we work with copies or exports.

Finance and operational accounting

The repetitive side of finance (recording invoices, balancing the bank, chasing payments, preparing the close) eats up hours that add no decisions. We do it with AI agents, and a person reviews every deliverable. You and your accountant still make the decisions and sign.

What we do

  • Reading supplier invoices from PDFs or photos and recording them in your spreadsheet or accounting software.
  • Bank reconciliation: matching the statement against invoices, receipts and payments, with an explained list of differences.
  • Collections control: ageing of balances, drafted payment reminders and follow-up.
  • Annual budget and cash-flow forecast based on your historical data.
  • Monthly close: income, expenses and margins by business line, cost centre or project.
  • Documentation requested by your accountancy firm (gestoría), your auditor or your bank, organised and complete.
  • Review of recurring expenses and subscriptions to spot duplicates and unnecessary charges.
  • Templates and automations in Excel, Google Sheets or your accounting software.

What we deliver

  • Import file or spreadsheet ready for your accounting software
  • Reconciliation report with outstanding items explained
  • Editable cash-flow forecast
  • Monthly close summary in PDF and spreadsheet
  • Written procedure so you can repeat the process
What we don't do. We do not file taxes or sign annual accounts or audit reports: that is for your tax adviser or a registered auditor. We prepare the groundwork so they need less time.
Do you work with my accounting software?

We work with standard exports and imports (Excel, CSV and the bank's transactions file) and with the programs most widely used in Spain. If your software supports imports, we leave the file ready to load; if not, we give you the spreadsheet in order.

Who sees my financial data?

The AI models we use, on a professional tier that does not train on your data, and the person who reviews the work. We sign a data processing agreement with you.

Human resources

HR generates a lot of paperwork: job ads, CV screening, welcome manuals, internal policies, mandatory records. We prepare that work with agents and human review; decisions about people remain yours.

What we do

  • Clear, bias-free job ads and role descriptions.
  • Candidate shortlisting using the criteria you set, with the reason for each assessment.
  • Welcome manual, onboarding plan and documentation for new joiners.
  • Internal policies: remote working, digital disconnection, use of AI, code of conduct.
  • Diagnosis and draft of the equality plan (a legal requirement in Spain for many companies), harassment protocol and LGBTI measures, ready to negotiate with workforce representatives.
  • Pay register and pay gap analysis based on your data.
  • Employee engagement surveys and their analysis.
  • Payroll data organised for your payroll adviser (gestoría) and monthly tracking of changes and incidents.

What we deliver

  • Job ads ready to publish
  • Shortlisting report with the reason for each assessment
  • Welcome manual in your format
  • Draft internal policies
  • Draft diagnosis and equality plan for the negotiating committee
What we don't do. Someone in your company always decides who gets hired: AI does not make decisions about people. We do not represent you before the Labour Inspectorate (Inspección de Trabajo) or in court, and we do not replace your labour relations adviser (graduado social).
Can AI be used to screen CVs?

Yes, with care: candidates must be informed, bias must be monitored and a person must decide. The EU AI Act treats recruitment as a high-risk use; that is why we apply your criteria, explain each assessment and leave the decision to you.

Do you produce the full equality plan?

We prepare the diagnosis, the draft plan and the protocols. The law requires it to be negotiated with the legal representatives of the workforce and then registered; your company does that using our documents.

Can you get me customers?

We prepare the material and data so your team can sell better, but we do not do cold outreach by email or phone.

Customer service

Many enquiries repeat themselves and others go unanswered for too long. We organise your customer service with AI agents: a knowledge base, draft replies and assistants that identify themselves as AI and hand over to a person.

What we do

  • Knowledge base and template answers built from your emails and frequently asked questions.
  • AI assistant for your website or email that answers using your information and hands over to a person anything it does not know.
  • Sorting of incoming messages by topic and urgency.
  • Draft replies so your team only has to review and send.
  • Support in several languages.
  • Analysis of complaints and claims to find their recurring causes.
  • Satisfaction surveys and their analysis.
  • Service procedures and templates for your team.

What we deliver

  • Organised knowledge base
  • Configured AI assistant, with a conversation log
  • Reply templates
  • Report on contact reasons and complaints
  • Procedure for handing over to a person
What we don't do. The assistant always identifies itself as AI and hands over to a person any complaint and anything it cannot resolve with your information.
Can the assistant make up answers?

We configure it to answer only with your information and to hand over to a person when it does not have it. At the start we review its conversations to correct it.

Do I have to change my customer service software?

Not necessarily: we work with your email, your website chat or your ticketing tool if it supports integrations.

Compliance and non-reserved legal work

Complying with regulations generates documents that someone has to draft, organise and keep up to date. We do that documentation work with AI agents and human review; legal decisions remain in the hands of your lawyer or adviser.

What we do

  • Record of processing activities, privacy notices and data protection risk analysis.
  • Internal policies: whistleblowing channel, use of AI, information security.
  • Inventory of the AI systems your organisation uses and AI literacy training.
  • Contract summaries and comparisons so your lawyer can review what matters.
  • Monitoring of regulatory changes that affect your sector.
  • Preparation for VERI*FACTU (Spain's tamper-proof invoicing system): choosing a compliant invoicing program and getting it running.
  • Accessibility statement and documentation for your website.
  • Internal documents based on templates your adviser has validated.

What we deliver

  • Record of processing activities
  • Internal policies and procedures
  • Inventory of AI systems
  • Contract summary with the key clauses flagged
  • Regulatory updates bulletin
What we don't do. We do not give legal or tax advice, we do not sign opinions and we do not represent anyone before courts or public authorities: that is for a lawyer or a registered adviser. We prepare the documentation and the groundwork.
Does this replace my lawyer?

No. We do the documentation work that currently costs your team or your firm hours; legal decisions and anything that requires a professional's signature stay in their hands.

What is AI literacy?

The EU AI Act requires people who use AI in an organisation to have sufficient training. We prepare that training, tailored to the tools you use.

Translation and languages

Working across Europe means documents, websites and videos in several languages. We translate with AI and your glossary, and a person reviews every deliverable before it is sent.

What we do

  • Translation of documents, websites and catalogues with human review.
  • Localisation of software and applications (PO, JSON and XLIFF files).
  • Subtitles for videos and their translation.
  • Reviewed transcription of audio and interviews.
  • Glossaries and translation memories for your organisation.
  • Review of existing translations.
  • Writing directly in another language, not literal translation.
  • Summaries in your language of documents written in others.

What we deliver

  • Translated document in its original format
  • Localisation files
  • SRT or VTT subtitles
  • Reviewed transcript
  • Organisation glossary
What we don't do. We do not provide sworn translation: documents that require it are signed by a sworn translator appointed by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
Which languages?

Spanish, Catalan, English, French, German, Italian and Portuguese, and other European languages. In the quote we tell you who reviews each language.

Machine or human translation?

Both: the first version is produced by AI using your glossary, and a person reviews it before delivery.
