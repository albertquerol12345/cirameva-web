How we work

Finished work, at a fixed price, done by AI agents and reviewed by a person.

Fixed price before we start

  • You tell us about the job and within 24 h we reply with the scope, deadline and price.
  • The price doesn't change if we take longer. If you change the job, we give you the new price before going on.
  • We don't sell hours or software subscriptions: we sell the delivered result.

AI agents, a checker and a person

  • AI agents do the work: they read, write, calculate, translate and code.
  • An automatic checker verifies every figure, calculation and quotation against its source and flags anything doubtful.
  • A person reviews the delivery before it is sent and answers for it.

Every job improves the next

  • Every job leaves behind its own recipe and checker.
  • That is why the next similar job comes out faster, cheaper and with fewer errors.

Physical work, with integrators

  • In robotics projects we do the office side: study, specification, comparison, documentation and training.
  • Installation, commissioning and CE marking are carried out and signed by a robotics integrator you choose.

You decide and sign

  • Decisions about your business, about people, and anything requiring a licensed professional's signature stay with you or your professional.
  • If we can't do a job well, we tell you in our reply and don't charge for it.

Payment

  • By bank transfer: 50% when you accept the quote and 50% on delivery.
  • Jobs under €100: 100% on acceptance. Monthly services: paid monthly in advance, no minimum term.

Data and confidentiality

  • We use your data only for your job and sign a data processing agreement when we process personal data on your behalf.
  • AI providers work on professional plans that do not train on your data. Details in the privacy policy (Spanish).
